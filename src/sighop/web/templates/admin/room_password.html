Rotating a password makes every existing member log in again with the new one. Members that do not are refused in silence, which is what the firmware does with a failed login — they will not be told why.

A field left blank leaves that password unchanged. An empty guest password is legal and must be an explicit choice, never a default — which is what the first checkbox above is.

The read-only fallback is the last step of a login: with it set, a password that matched neither the administrator's nor the guest's is answered with read-only access — the peer receives history and may not post. Without it, such a login is answered with silence, which is what the firmware does.
